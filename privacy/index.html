Privacy Policy

GPN247 Uploader (the "App"), operated by the owner of the GeopoliticalNews247 channel.

1. What the App is

The App is a private internal tool used only by the channel owner to upload, schedule and organise videos (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, playlists) on the owner's own YouTube channel. It has no other users.

2. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data the App accesses

With the owner's OAuth authorisation, the App accesses only the owner's YouTube channel to: upload videos, set video metadata and thumbnails, schedule publishing, and create or update playlists. It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

4. How data is stored

The only stored credential is the owner's OAuth refresh token, kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and used solely by the owner's automated workflow. Video files and metadata are generated by the owner and are not shared with third parties other than YouTube.

5. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share any personal data. No cookies, analytics or tracking are used by the App.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Deleting the stored secret removes the App's access permanently.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: message @geopoliticalnews247 on Instagram or via the YouTube channel page.
